## 🛡️ Security Architecture

GeoAttend treats the client device as an untrusted environment, layering several "Proof-of-Physical-Presence" checks:

### 🔬 Physics-Based Anti-Spoofing
Standard `isMock()` flags can be bypassed by Xposed/Magisk. GeoAttend detects these by analyzing physical anomalies:
*   **Velocity Sanity**: High-speed "teleportation" detection (>50 m/s).
*   **Accuracy Variance**: Identifying suspiciously "perfect" (constant 1.0m) GPS signals.
*   **Altitude Noise**: Real GPS sensors have altitude variance; constant 0.0m is flagged.
*   **Inter-Session Movement**: Validates plausibility of distance/time since the last app session.
